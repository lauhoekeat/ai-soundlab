## Local formative rubric — product proposal, not official TP rubric

Score each mission's explanation 0–2: 0 missing/contradictory; 1 relevant claim with weak observation; 2 correct claim supported by a relevant observation. Maximum 8. Record hints and revisions separately; do not subtract for disability, sample use, AI uncertainty or technical failure. The final report additionally asks for one limitation of AI and one improvement to an investigation; teacher reviews these descriptively rather than inflating a game score.

M1 full-credit anchor: identifies an observed vibrating part and links it to sound; AI label alone earns no science credit. M2: evidence from listeners in multiple directions. M3: returning sound and a reflection setting. M4: two feasible context-aware interventions preserving essential warnings. Exact Malay anchors must be teacher-authored/reviewed before use.

## Learning evidence plan

Five short pre/post items use matched concepts with different examples: cause of sound; listeners around a source; echo explanation; contextual usefulness; noise-reduction reasoning. Teacher prepares answer keys before pilot. Compare paired anonymous scores, report denominator, median change and range. Target median gain ≥1/5 is a formative signal, not a promised result or causal proof. No invented charts or “X% improvement” in competition material.

Teacher observation checklist: predicts before acting; varies one condition; distinguishes observation from model suggestion; revises using evidence; explains a tradeoff. Learners who cannot hear or operate the mic use described samples and teacher-recorded oral/selected-response explanations. Report the accommodation, not a lower science expectation.

Before pilot signoff: curriculum edition/stream and page; BM copy reviewer; suitable objects/activity; lesson timing; allowed pupil evidence; rubric anchor review. These signoffs remain pending.
